    Create MRI Slots

    MRI slots are only created in bulk, via Add MRI Slots in the sidebar — this keeps every MRI slot going through the same overlap/conflict checks and, if you choose, lets you push it straight into a Day 1/Day 2 schedule in the same step.

    Add Day 2 Slots

    Add one or more Day 2 slots without building a new MRI/Day 1 schedule. Each row must be 22–26 hours after at least one existing available Day 1 slot, and must not conflict with any MRI, Day 1, or Day 2 slot (booked or unbooked) — validated when you click Create All. Assigned Staff is required per row.
